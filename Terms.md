## 2. The Service

Fliphone connects Discord channels from different servers for real time conversations.

Fliphone provides random 1:1 calls, group rooms for up to five servers, message relay through Discord webhooks, supported reactions, reviewed GIF and custom emoji relay, user profiles, optional notifications, blocking, reporting, filtering, and moderation controls.

An administrator must complete `/setup` in a chosen text channel before users can start conversations there.

## 3. Identity and External Contact

Fliphone generally hides the identity of a connected server.

By default, relayed messages may show the sender's filtered Discord display name and avatar. Individual users may enable anonymous mode to use a stable Fliphone identity instead.

Users may voluntarily share their Discord username with `/friendrequest`. Fliphone cannot guarantee the identity, age, intentions, or behavior of another user. Use friend requests and contact outside Fliphone at your own risk.

You must not impersonate another person or use deceptive identities for harassment, fraud, or abuse.

Reports must contain an honest explanation. Users may provide a relevant media link. False, abusive, misleading, or spam reports are prohibited.

When a report is submitted, Fliphone may fetch limited recent context from the reporting Discord channel and send it to a private Discord moderation channel. The report process and retention rules are described in the Privacy Policy.

Authorized Fliphone moderators may review reports, resolve reports, restrict users or servers, maintain safety lists, remove reviewed content, and report serious violations to Discord or relevant authorities.

Moderation action may be taken without advance notice when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or Discord communities. A user may contact the support server to contest an action, but reversal is not guaranteed.

Administrators may use `/setup`, `/check`, `/repair`, `/kick`, `/block`, and `/teardown` to manage the service. `/teardown` uses a Discord button confirmation and requires Manage Channels permission.
